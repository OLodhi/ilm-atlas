# Ilm Atlas

**Your digital companion for exploring authentic Islamic knowledge**

Ilm Atlas (Arabic: علم أطلس — "Atlas of Knowledge") is an AI-powered research tool that helps Muslims find answers grounded in the Quran, the six major Hadith collections, and classical Tafsir (Quranic commentary) — all with full source citations so you can verify everything yourself.

> **Try it now:** [ilm-atlas.com](https://ilm-atlas.com)

---

## Why Ilm Atlas?

Finding reliable Islamic information online can be overwhelming. Search engines return thousands of results from unknown sources, and it's hard to know what's authentic. Ilm Atlas solves this by:

- **Only using verified primary sources** — the Quran, the six authentic Hadith collections (Kutub al-Sittah), and respected classical Tafsir works
- **Showing you the exact source** for every claim — click any citation to see the original Arabic text alongside its English translation
- **Never making things up** — the AI only answers based on what the sources actually say, never inventing rulings or opinions
- **Maintaining proper Islamic etiquette (Adab)** — using honorifics (SAW, RA, RH), never issuing personal fatwas, and presenting scholarly differences fairly

---

## What Can You Ask?

Ask any question about Islam in plain English (or Arabic), and Ilm Atlas will search over 76,000 primary source texts to give you an answer with citations. For example:

- *"What does Islam say about patience during hardship?"*
- *"What are the rights of a wife in Islam?"*
- *"How many times is Prophet Isa (AS) mentioned in the Quran?"*
- *"What is Surah Al-Kahf about?"*
- *"Explain the conditions of Zakat"*
- *"What did the Prophet (SAW) say about treating neighbours?"*

You can ask follow-up questions too — the conversation keeps context, so you can go deeper into any topic naturally.

## Features

### Chat with Sources
Ask questions and receive well-structured answers with numbered source citations. Every claim is backed by a specific Quran ayah, Hadith, or Tafsir passage that you can click to read in full.

### Read the Sources Directly
Browse the full text of the Quran, all six Hadith collections, and five classical Tafsir works in a clean, easy-to-read format — like having an entire Islamic library at your fingertips.

### Arabic and English Side by Side
Every source is presented with the original Arabic text (in Uthmani script for the Quran) alongside the English translation. Arabic text is displayed right-to-left with beautiful traditional typography.

### Auto-Translation
Arabic-only sources (such as Tafsir Al-Jalalayn, Al-Qurtubi, and Al-Sa'di) can be translated to English with one click, so language is never a barrier to understanding.

### Respectful and Trustworthy
The system is designed with Islamic etiquette built in:
- Uses proper honorifics throughout (&#xFDFA; for the Prophet, Radi Allahu Anhu for Companions)
- Never issues personal fatwas or religious verdicts
- When scholars have differed, it presents the mainstream Sunni positions fairly
- Always prioritises Quran first, then Hadith, following the source hierarchy

## How It Works

1. **You ask a question** — type your question in English or Arabic
2. **Sources are searched** — the system searches across all 76,000+ passages to find the most relevant texts
3. **An answer is generated** — AI reads the retrieved sources and composes an answer based solely on what they say
4. **Citations are provided** — every claim links back to the exact source, so you can verify it yourself

The AI never generates information from its own "knowledge" — it only uses the retrieved source texts. If the sources don't contain an answer, it will tell you honestly rather than guessing.

---

## Important Disclaimer

Ilm Atlas is a **research and educational tool**. It is not a substitute for consulting qualified Islamic scholars (Ulama) for personal religious guidance, especially on matters of Fiqh (Islamic jurisprudence) and personal fatwas. Always consult a knowledgeable scholar for specific rulings that affect your worship and daily life.

The source texts are publicly available from their respective repositories and APIs. Ilm Atlas simply makes them easier to search and cross-reference.

## Aqeedah (Creed)

Ilm Atlas follows the creed and methodology of **Ahle-us-Sunnah wal Jama'ah** — grounded in the Quran, the authentic Sunnah, and the understanding of the Sahaba (Companions) and Tabi'un (their successors). When presenting scholarly positions, it defaults to mainstream Sunni consensus.
